What is AI?

AI is technology that helps computers perform tasks that normally need human intelligence, such as learning, recognizing patterns and understanding language.

📊

Machine Learning

A part of AI where computers learn patterns from data and use those patterns to make useful predictions or decisions.

💬

Natural Language

AI can process human language. Chatbots and voice assistants are common examples.

👁️

Computer Vision

AI can analyze pictures and videos to identify objects, faces, text and other information.

🌍 AI Around Us

AI is already part of many everyday technologies.

🎓

Education

Study assistants, personalized learning and educational tools.

🏥

Healthcare

AI can support medical research and help analyze large amounts of information.

🎬

Entertainment

Recommendations for movies, music, games and other content.

🚗

Transport

Navigation, traffic prediction and driver-assistance technologies.

⚡ Benefits & Responsibilities

✨ Benefits

  • Automates repetitive work
  • Processes large amounts of data quickly
  • Supports learning and research
  • Helps solve complex problems

🛡️ Responsible AI

  • Check information before trusting it
  • Protect personal information
  • Think about fairness and bias
  • Keep humans involved in important decisions
